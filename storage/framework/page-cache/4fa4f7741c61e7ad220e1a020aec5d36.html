Canada's Trusted Wholesale HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical Distributor

BHS Supplies is Mississauga's premier wholesale distributor serving HVAC contractors, plumbers, electricians, and building trades professionals across Canada. We stock thousands of SKUs from leading manufacturers — available for same-day pickup or fast national shipping.

HVAC Supplies

Air conditioners, furnaces, heat pumps, ductwork, thermostats, refrigerants, coils, blower motors, and all HVAC parts and accessories. We carry residential and commercial HVAC equipment at wholesale prices.

Plumbing Supplies

Pipes, fittings, valves, water heaters, pumps, backflow preventers, and plumbing fixtures. Whether you need copper, PVC, or PEX materials, we have everything a licensed plumber needs on the job.

Electrical Supplies

Wire, conduit, panels, breakers, outlets, switches, lighting, and electrical accessories. Our electrical inventory is stocked for contractors and electricians at competitive wholesale rates.
